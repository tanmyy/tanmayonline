About

A Creative with Range

I'm a video editor and graphic designer focused on content that feels current, clean, and useful for real business goals. My work spans branding, digital content design, short-form video editing, company profiles, brochures, product visuals, and promotional creatives.

I work platform-native—using Canva, CapCut and Nano Banana Pro to move fast without sacrificing polish. That means less back-and-forth, faster turnaround, and content built for how people actually consume it online.

Open to freelance projects, remote work, and creative roles in Toronto, Mississauga, and beyond.
